## Observations

These statements are supported directly by the figures above.

1. Mortgage Servicing is the largest category at 25.8% of complaints.
2. Most complaints are still open (86 of 120). Escalated and In Review together make up 62 complaints.
3. Among closed cases, Account Access had the longest average resolution (16.2 days, n=5) and Service Quality the shortest (6.2 days, n=6).
4. Resolution averages for Card or Transaction Dispute and Digital Banking rest on fewer than 5 closed cases. Treat them as indicative only.

## What this analysis does not show

- **No trends.** This is a single snapshot. The numbers say nothing about whether volume or resolution time is rising or falling.
- **No causes.** Category volume does not explain *why* customers complained.
- **Resolution time covers closed cases only.** It says nothing about how long the open complaints (86) will take, and the averages may change as those cases close.
- The source data is synthetic training data.
